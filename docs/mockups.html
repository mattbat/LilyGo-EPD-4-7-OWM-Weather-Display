Tempest e-ink display: three layout options

Each screen below is drawn at the panel's real pixel size, using only the greys the panel can show. The numbers are sample data, not your station's. Big type is Atkinson Hyperlegible, a font designed for low-vision reading, which converts cleanly to the e-paper font format.

Chosen · Option C, extended

Past & Future, with a smart slot

Two moments on the same layout. The black box on the right is the smart slot: it shows whatever matters most right now. The line along the bottom adds sun times, dew point, rain totals and station health.

A quiet afternoon

An evening thunderstorm

Smart slot priority

  • Lightning in the last 3 h: strike count and nearest distance
  • Raining now: rain rate
  • UV 6 or higher: UV warning
  • Otherwise: when the forecast says rain is next

New on the chart

  • Lightning row: a bolt and strike count under each hour with strikes
  • Rain bars auto-scale, so a downpour and a drizzle both read well

Bottom line

Sunrise and sunset, dew point, rain this month and this year, and station battery. Wind now shows gusts under the speed.

Overnight · 10 PM to 5 AM

Overnight summary

The 10 PM update draws this screen instead of the live one, then the board sleeps until 5 AM. The black header bar and the moon make it obvious from across the room that nothing on it is live. It shows how today went and what tomorrow's forecast says.

Low battery · below 3.3 V

Charge me

When the 18650 drops below 3.3 V, the board draws this screen and stops connecting to WiFi. Every two hours it wakes for a fraction of a second to check the voltage, and returns to normal once the battery is charged.

The original three options

Option A

Big & Simple

Idea

One huge temperature you can read from across the room, four key readings, and one 24-hour chart of temperature and rain.

Shows

  • Now: temp, feels-like, high/low, conditions
  • Wind, rain today, humidity, pressure trend
  • Last 24 h: temperature line + hourly rain bars

Trade-off

No multi-day forecast. Most readable of the three. Easiest to build

Option B

Tile Dashboard

Idea

Six sensor tiles, each with a large reading and its own 24-hour sparkline, over a 5-day forecast strip.

Shows

  • Temp, wind, rain, humidity, pressure, sun/UV
  • A trend line for every sensor
  • 5-day forecast: icon, high/low, rain chance

Trade-off

Shows the most data. Numbers are smaller (about 56 px), so it reads best within a couple of metres.

Option C

Past & Future Timeline

Idea

A wide 48-hour chart: what your station measured over the last 24 hours, joined at "now" to the Tempest forecast for the next 24.

Shows

  • Now: temp and four key readings
  • Observed temp (solid) and forecast temp (dashed), with highs and lows labelled
  • Measured rain (black bars) and rain chance (grey bars)

Trade-off

The most distinctive chart. Uses the Tempest forecast API on top of station history. Most data per glance

Pieces can be mixed: for example A's header with C's chart, or B's forecast strip under A.

E-ink note: grey fills render as true greys on this panel. Lines are at least 2 px wide so they stay crisp, and nothing relies on colour.
